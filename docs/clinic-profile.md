# TrueGuard Labs — Profile

## Overview

TrueGuard Labs is a product/service of **Koby Soft**, focused on independently auditing AI services for companies and individuals.

## Relationship to Other Koby Soft Products

TrueGuard Labs shares a parent company (Koby Soft) with other Koby Soft products, including **Rentflow** and **Dentflow**, but otherwise operates independently:

- No shared codebase, data, or direct product integration with Rentflow or Dentflow.
- No shared branding beyond the common parent-company relationship.
- Distinct target audience: TrueGuard Labs serves companies and individuals seeking independent AI audits, rather than the domains Rentflow and Dentflow address.

## What TrueGuard Labs Does

TrueGuard Labs performs independent audits of AI services on behalf of its clients, evaluating them for companies and individuals who need an outside assessment of an AI system or product.
